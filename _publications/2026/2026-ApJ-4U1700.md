abstract: >-
  Utilizing NuSTAR's stray light, we studied additional observations of 4U 1700-377 in order to better understand its variable spectrum and possible emission and absorption features. We find no evidence for a cyclotron resonant scattering feature (CRSF), as well as show that even longer timescale variability ($\sim 50$ ks) could still be explained by density and temperature variability in the companion's stellar wind.
