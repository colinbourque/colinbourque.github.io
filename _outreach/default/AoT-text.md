<div class="p-4">
    <h5>Astronomy on Tap</h5>
    <hr />
    <p>
      Astronomy on Tap brings astronomy to a greater audience through presentations at bars across the world. In 2026, I spoke at Astronomy on Tap: Vermont at Four Quarters Brewing in Winooski, describing how we can distinguish between neutron stars and black holes in X-ray binaries.
    </p>
    <p>
      In this general-audience talk I explained how the strong magnetic field of a neutron star funnels material onto its poles through columns, and how this leads to distinct observable characteristics. With some bespoke animations I attempted to make the concepts of pulsation searches and cyclotron resonant scattering accessible to non-astronomers, and also made sure that people could leave with some important take-aways about how to diagnose the next X-ray binary they encounter in the wild.
    </p>
